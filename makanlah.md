# Makanlah

## Restorans and Kopitiams

1. **Oriental Kopi**  
   Egg tarts and peanut butter toast is a must.

2. **Wang Yee Wang Restaurant**  
   Claypot Yee Mee is a must.

3. **Alisan Kopitiam**  
   Wan Tan Mee is a must.

4. **Nanyang Cafe**  
   It used to be good, but its too expensive. Only thing 'worth' getting is their buns.

5. **Restoran K.T.L**  
   A place that sells good steamed food at a reasonable price. Their fish, egg, chicken are dope.

6. **25 and 26 River Seafood Restaurant** (Bentong)  
   A good place in Bentong to get good tai chow. Price is reasonable too.

7. **Meng Kee Wan Tan Mee** (Bentong)  
   Legit one of the best WTM I've had in Bentong.

8. **Restoran Congee Rice Noodles** (Melaka)  
   Expensive, but the food they serve is legit good.

**Trylist:**
- Yong Hua Restaurant (WTM)

## Food Court

1. **Bachang Garden Avenue** (Melaka)  
   Eatable food court in Melaka.

## Ayam Gepuk

1. **Ayam Gepuk Artisan**  
   By far the best Ayam Gepuk chain for me. Their fried chicken is on the same level as MCD's, but MCD wins by crispiness. A good chain if you like fried chicken instead of the classic chicken like me.

2. **Ayam Gepuk Tok Mat**  
   Taste quite similar to Artisan, but their peanut sauce tastes better than Artisan.

**Trylist:**
- Gepuklah

## Nasi Lemak

1. **Nasi Lemak Angah**  
   By far the best nasi lemak, sambal is on the sweeter, sour and spicy side. The owner is an ex-bartender, which is something new for me. Only thing I would complain is that the ayam rendang portion is small and its very far from my place.

2. **Nasi Lemak Burung Hantu**  
   I would say that Burung Hantu wins Village Park in terms of their sambal cuz they are more dominant in terms of spiciness.

3. **Village Park Restaurant**  
   Overhyped, but still eatable. Their sambal and rendang is okay, its on the sweeter side.

## Pastries

1. **Verrona Hills Bread & Patisserie**  
   The CUMBREAD is a must.

2. **Rotiboy**  
   Self-explanatory.

## Bak Kut Teh

1. **Teluk Pulai Bak Kut Teh** (Klang)

## Dessert

1. **Lubiantan ABC**  
   The closest place I can get an ABC, taste okay but it's a bit pricey.

2. **Tak Ice Cream Corner** (Bentong)  
   Good ice cream place in Bentong. Not too expensive and a lot of choices to choose from.

3. **Kwong Wah Ais Kacang**
   Saw JBS went here and tried it. Their ABC Gula Melaka is dope. Tried rojak too, its good ngl, but too bad I'm picky and only some of the fruits. Price is a little expensive but overall, the place is decent. Not a place I would come everyday though.

## Pan Mee

1. **Super Kitchen Chili Pan Mee**  
   A good restaurant chain that sells good Pan Mee. But each branches has different tastes.

**Trylist:**
- Jojo Little Kitchen

## Beef Noodle

1. **Hing Loong Taiwanese Noodle**  
   Big portion, thick broth. Dope.

## Asam Laksa

1. **Asam Laksa Petaling Street**  
   There's no stall name but it's easy to miss too. They have thick and sour broth and gives out good portion on the fish.

## Wan Tan Mee

1. **Wan Tan Mee Petaling Street**  
   Yet another place with no stall name but it's easy to miss too. One of the food that is good in Chinatown.

## Soya Bean

1. **Kim Soya Bean**  
   The stall is right in the middle of Chinatown and it's hard to miss. Their soya bean taste dope.

## Filipino Food

1. **Mabuhay Laguna Restaurant**  
   The only place in KL where you can get authentic PH food. Food legit taste good but it's a bit pricey. But its fucking worth it.

## Nyonya Food

1. **Restoran Baba Albert** (Melaka)  
   My first experience with Nyonya food. feels legit and authentic.
